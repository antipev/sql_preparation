# Generate Vibe Coding Product Ideas

**Category:** Coding

## Description

Generate profitable product ideas with this AI prompt, featuring success ranking, viability analysis, and personalized recommendations for vibe coding projects.

## What This Prompt Does

● Guides through a 5-question discovery process to understand your vibe-coding style and goals.

● Gathers information about your skills, interests, market knowledge, and product preferences through targeted questions.

● Delivers a ranked list of product ideas with success potential analysis and personalized fit explanations.
