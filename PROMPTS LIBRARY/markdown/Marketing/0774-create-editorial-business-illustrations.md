# Create Editorial Business Illustrations

**Category:** Marketing

## Description

Generate modern editorial illustrations with this AI prompt, featuring simplified figures, geometric elements, and strategic color accents for business publications.

## What This Prompt Does

● Creates a modern editorial AI image with simplified human figures and geometric shapes representing business concepts.
● Uses thin black line art with limited flat color accents in blue, green, and yellow against a white background.
● Generates a vertical composition featuring symbolic objects that convey growth and progress for financial publications.

## Prompt

```text
{
  "concept": "[CONCEPT]",
  "main_action": "[MAIN ACTION]",
  "symbol_object": "[SYMBOL OBJECT]",
  "accent_colors": ["blue", "green", "yellow"],
  "prompt": "Create a modern editorial illustration representing [CONCEPT]. The scene shows [MAIN ACTION] in a symbolic, metaphor-driven composition. Human figures are simplified and abstract, drawn with thin black line art and neutral, professional expressions. Architectural blocks and financial elements like charts or bars are geometric and minimal. At the top of the composition, include [SYMBOL OBJECT] as a metaphor for growth and progress. Use a limited color palette with flat accent colors {{accent_colors}} applied only to selected elements, while most of the illustration remains black and white. No gradients, no shadows. Clean white background. The overall tone should feel optimistic, structured, and suitable for financial or business editorial use.",

  "negative_prompt": "photorealistic, 3D render, realistic anatomy, detailed faces, gradients, shadows, neon colors, playful cartoon style, cluttered layout",

  "style": "editorial conceptual illustration",

  "line_style": {
    "color": "black",
    "weight": "thin and consistent",
    "feel": "precise and clean"
  },

  "character_style": {
    "proportions": "simplified and geometric",
    "faces": "minimal features, neutral expressions"
  },

  "color_rules": {
    "palette": "mostly black and white",
    "accents": "flat solid colors only, restrained usage"
  },

  "composition": {
    "layout": "vertical hierarchy",
    "focus": "growth, balance, and [CONCEPT] progress"
  },

  "background": "pure white",

  "usage": "financial editorial, business magazines, annual reports, investor presentations",

  "resolution": "1024x1024"
}
```
