**Category:** Marketing

## Description

Create premium product advertisements with this AI prompt, featuring grid layouts, studio photography, customization highlights, lifestyle scenes, and modern branding elements.

## What This Prompt Does

● Generates a premium 2×3 grid AI image showcasing a tech product across six panels with different angles and settings.
● Creates professional product photography scenes including hero shots, macro details, illumination effects, and lifestyle desk setups.
● Produces high-quality AI images optimized for social media ads and e-commerce with studio lighting and modern aesthetics.
