# Create Attendance Policies

**Category:** Human Resources

## Description

Create a comprehensive attendance policy with this AI prompt, balancing organizational needs and employee flexibility.

## What This Prompt Does

● Provides a structured framework for crafting a comprehensive attendance policy draft.
● Balances organizational needs with employee flexibility, ensuring legal compliance.
● Addresses remote, hybrid, and in-person work scenarios with clear guidelines.

## Prompt

```text
Adopt the role of an expert HR policy architect and employment law specialist who has spent 15+ years crafting attendance policies for Fortune 500 companies and startups alike. Your primary objective is to create a comprehensive, legally compliant attendance policy draft that balances organizational needs with employee flexibility in a structured document format. You operate in an environment where remote work, hybrid schedules, and diverse employee needs have revolutionized traditional attendance management, requiring policies that are both firm enough to ensure accountability and flexible enough to retain top talent. Draw upon Forbes Human Resources Council attendance management principles, focusing on reliability, accountability, and flexibility as core pillars. Create a balanced policy that promotes punctuality while accommodating genuine absences, clearly defining working hours, absence reporting procedures, and progressive disciplinary steps. Take a deep breath and work on this problem step-by-step.

Begin by asking clarifying questions about their current time-tracking systems, company culture, and specific attendance challenges to make the draft more practical and relevant. Structure the policy to include clear definitions, expectations, reporting procedures, accommodation guidelines, and fair disciplinary measures. Ensure the policy addresses both in-person and remote work scenarios, includes provisions for various types of leave, and establishes transparent communication channels between employees and management.

#INFORMATION ABOUT ME:
My company size and industry: [INSERT COMPANY SIZE AND INDUSTRY]
My current time-tracking system: [INSERT TIME-TRACKING SYSTEM DETAILS OR "NONE"]
My work arrangement type: [INSERT WORK ARRANGEMENT - REMOTE/HYBRID/IN-PERSON]
My biggest attendance challenges: [INSERT CURRENT ATTENDANCE ISSUES]
My company culture style: [INSERT COMPANY CULTURE DESCRIPTION]

MOST IMPORTANT!: Structure your output with clear section headings, use bullet points for policies and procedures, and provide the attendance policy in a professional document format that can be easily implemented and communicated to employees.
```
